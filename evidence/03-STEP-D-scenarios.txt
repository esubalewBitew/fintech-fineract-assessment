Step 3 / Part D — Lifecycle to Closed
=====================================
Date: 2026-10-29
Evidence: evidence/03-lifecycle.json

Flow
----
POST /clients → POST /loans/apply → POST /loans/{ref}/approve
→ POST /loans/disburse → 3 partial repayments → payoff rounds until outstanding 0
→ final status Closed (obligations met)

Happy
-----

H3.2 Approve — Approved
H3.3 Disburse — Active; principal outstanding ≈ disbursed
H3.4 Repayments 1–3 — outstanding decreases each step (logged in JSON)
H3.5 Payoff — Closed (obligations met); totalOutstanding 0
H3.6 Snapshot — 03-lifecycle.json contains every step + balances
